About the club

A team that cheers for every kid

Stanley Wrestling Club is a youth program run by volunteer coaches and parents. We teach kids the sport in a safe, organized room, build confidence and discipline, and make sure everyone feels part of the team, whatever their experience level.

Practice is drop-off only: drop your wrestler off at the start and pick them up at the end.

Common questions

What should my wrestler bring?

Water bottle, athletic shorts and a t-shirt, and wrestling shoes if they have them. No jewelry. Coaches will share gear details on Heja.

What happens at practice?

Warm-up, technique, drilling and live wrestling, with a short cool-down. Coaches adjust the pace for each age group.

When are the meets and tournaments?

Home duals are Dec 3 and Dec 10. The Rumble on Red in Fargo is Dec 30–31. See the Schedule tab. More tournaments are added as they are announced.

What does it cost after preseason?

Monthly dues, club gear, and an entry fee for each tournament. Amounts will be posted here and on Heja once the board sets them.

Are the coaches background checked?

Yes. Every SWC coach completes the mandatory USA Wrestling background screening.

Wrestling 101: how does a tournament day work?

Wrestlers are grouped by age, weight and experience. After weigh-ins, each wrestler gets a bracket and a mat number, then waits to be called for each match. Days can be long, so bring snacks, water and something to do. Coaches will walk new families through the first one.

Where can I find or pass on used gear?

Outgrown shoes, headgear or singlets? Post them on Heja or tell a coach so another family can use them.

What about injuries or medical needs?

List allergies and medical notes on the registration form. Tell a coach at drop-off about anything new.

Can parents stay and watch?

No. Practice is drop-off only. Please drop your wrestler off at 4:00 PM and pick them up at 5:15 PM. Make sure a coach knows your wrestler has arrived, and keep your phone on in case we need to reach you.
